Hi, I'm Mathias. I work at the intersection of project management, IT consulting and engineering, where requirements turn into working systems.

Work

I started out in project management, then spent almost nine years in industrial software development, working in automation, robotics, MES and standard software. Today I bring both together: I lead projects, advise on IT topics and still enjoy getting hands-on with the technology.

Focus areas

  • Requirements engineering: gathering requirements, writing specifications, evaluating vendors
  • Data pipelines & IoT: MQTT, OPC UA, InfluxDB, Microsoft Fabric, Grafana and Prometheus, CAN/MDF4
  • Agentic engineering and AI-assisted development
  • DevOps & cloud: Azure, GitHub Actions, infrastructure as code with Bicep

Personal

Away from work, I'm happiest on my motorcycle, on adventure trips through the Balkans, Albania or along the Trans Euro Trail. At home I tinker with home automation: I built my own solar control system, running on a Raspberry Pi with Node-RED and MQTT. I also enjoy 3D printing, play basketball and have recently taken up golf.
